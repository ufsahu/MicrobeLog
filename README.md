#MicrobeLog

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is one of the most serious public threats globally for humans (Identified by the WHO, the UN & the World Economic Forum)

Tyks Laboratories in Turku, processes the antimicrobial susceptibility testing and analyses bacterial resistance, by developing annual reports

AMR annual hospital-level reports are still lacking details and imprecise data

Doctors require up-to-date, ward-specific bacterial susceptibility data to provide antibiotic treatments
