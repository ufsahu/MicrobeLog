**MicrobeLog**


Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is one of the most serious public threats globally for humans (Identified by the WHO, the UN & the World Economic Forum)

Tyks Laboratories in Turku, processes the antimicrobial susceptibility testing and analyses bacterial resistance, by developing annual reports

AMR annual hospital-level reports are still lacking details and imprecise data

MicrobeLog is a web/desktop and app-friendly dashboard, helping hospital staff members, R&D professionals, and patients can easily navigate through antimicrobial resistance (AMR) data. Doctors require up-to-date, ward-specific bacterial susceptibility data to provide antibiotic treatments. This is why, in our product, the user can easily navigate through multiple features of patient and microbial data to understand the specific outcome and predicted outcomes. 

We have also added an AI ask and Microscopy feature, so that any questions can be asked in the AI ask feature, and R&D professionals can easily analyse the data in the microscopy feature with an analysis tool extension called ImageJ.

The data can also be exported for statistical analysis purposes. 

The product is made with Lovable
